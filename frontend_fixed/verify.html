A family where all members fall in the OOD red zone is not yet trustworthy, no matter how often it is cited.

Why coverage matters more than accuracy. A model can score a high R² and still be dangerously overconfident: several benchmarks below fit the trend (R² > 0.9) while their 95% interval covers almost nothing. SwarmLabs normalizes the noise floor to 3% and never lowers it — lowering it would manufacture confidence.

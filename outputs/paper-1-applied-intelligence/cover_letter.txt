Summary of contribution
-----------------------
AI for Science (AI4S) systems increasingly promise to replace real
experiments with virtual ones. However, existing verification and
validation (V&V) frameworks anchor predictions on a single dimension
("is there a wet-lab result?"), which silently permits cross-system,
cross-version, and cross-condition anchor transfers that would be
rejected under careful human review. This is especially relevant
given that even frontier systems such as RayGun report verification
coverage of only 2.2 x 10^-5 (11 of 500,000 candidates reached
wet-lab confirmation; Nature 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10842-8).

We present the Three-Dimensional Evidence Anchoring (TDEA) framework,
which (i) lifts every anchor from a 1-D flag to a triple of
(experiment system, reagent/model version, operating condition),
(ii) enforces via 42 fail-closed verification gates -- 22 of which
are negation tests -- that incomplete or misaligned anchors cannot
trigger automatic decisions, and (iii) adds a layer of 5 independent
business-invariant assertions that operate outside the pipeline, so
that shared-defect truncation (for example, a missing optional
dependency that silently drops scenarios) is caught.

On the 95-scenario SwarmLabs primary benchmark, TDEA yields a
verification distribution of 91 PASS / 3 MARGINAL / 1 REFUTED, with
the single REFUTED case (mol_suzuki_yield) independently caught by
three distinct gates. All 7 decision-relevant anchors are fully
three-dimensional.

We go a step further and grade the 91 PASS scenarios by evidence
completeness, distinguishing 5 Pass_Full / 83 Pass_Partial / 3
Pass_Weak. This graded audit is the honest answer to the "PASS but
what does PASS actually mean" question any rigorous reviewer will ask.
We also disclose 13 GP-PASS scenarios that are refuted by a
structurally different surrogate as model-family dependent, and a
second-backend replication (a depth-30 random forest trained at 5x the
GP compute) confirms 82 of 95 scenarios in the same direction as the
GP, with the remaining 13 exactly matching the disclosed
model-family-dependent set.

Fit to Applied Intelligence
---------------------------
Applied Intelligence publishes original research at the intersection
of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and applied
intelligence systems, with explicit attention to trustworthiness and
reproducibility. TDEA is a policy layer -- not a new surrogate model
and not a new benchmark -- that constrains which in-silico
predictions may be trusted on the basis of the anchors that exist.
This policy layer is directly applicable to any AI4S platform that
operates a surrogate model plus a real-experiment anchor corpus,
which covers a large share of the journal's recent scope.

Originality and reproducibility
-------------------------------
- Full source code: https://github.com/lm203688/swarmlabs
- Public V&V dashboard: https://swarmlabs.tools/reports/
- All 42 gates, 5 invariants, 6 preflight checks, and 95-scenario
  benchmark are released under the MIT licence.
- The 19-anchor corpus is transcribed from existing notes and
  sources, not newly invented.
- The framework is surrogate-agnostic (Gaussian Process, deep
  ensembles, or Bayesian optimisation all fit).
